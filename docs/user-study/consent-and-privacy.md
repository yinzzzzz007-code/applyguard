# ApplyGuard formative study: consent and privacy

## Study purpose

We are collecting formative feedback about job-application workflows and the proposed evidence-traceability and human-confirmation concepts in ApplyGuard.

## Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. Participants may skip any question, stop at any time, and decline anonymous quotation without penalty.

## Data collected

The study records only anonymous responses about workflow, tools, time estimates, concerns, preferences, and critical failure cases. Participants are labeled `P01`, `P02`, and so on.

## Data not collected

Do not collect names, phone numbers, personal emails, student IDs, real resumes, identity documents, employer names, ATS accounts, login credentials, application screenshots, or sensitive eligibility information.

## Anonymous quotation

Ask separately: `May the team use a short anonymous quotation in project materials?` Choices: no quotation / anonymous quotation allowed. Never use a quotation that could identify the speaker.

## Interview notes

Use anonymized written notes only. Do not make audio or video recordings, photograph, or retain a participant's resume, JD, ATS page, or other artifacts.

## Storage and reporting

Keep notes in the project response template, separate any temporary scheduling contact, and remove accidental identifiers before analysis. Report only aggregated or anonymized findings actually collected. Do not fabricate participant counts, percentages, quotations, SUS scores, or conclusions.

## Consent record

`I have read this notice. I am eligible for the study. I voluntarily agree to provide anonymous feedback.` Yes / No
