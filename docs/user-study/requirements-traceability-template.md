## Handoff rules

- B supplies the anonymized finding and evidence reference.
- A converts accepted findings into formal requirements, priority, and acceptance criteria.
- C maps failure cases to Claim Validator, Risk Check, fallback, and failure tests.
- D reviews research material and may add only actual preliminary findings to the proposal slides.
