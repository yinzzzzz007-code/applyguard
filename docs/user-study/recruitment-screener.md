# ApplyGuard formative study: recruitment screener

## Purpose

This formative study examines how eligible technical-job applicants organize experience, tailor resumes, and decide when AI assistance needs human confirmation. It does not evaluate completed product performance.

## Eligibility

Recruit only a participant who answers **yes** to all four questions:

1. Are you a senior undergraduate or postgraduate student?
2. Are you currently concentrating applications for software, AI, or data roles?
3. Do you review or apply to at least five roles in a typical week during this search period?
4. Do you have at least three project, research, competition, or internship experiences that you could draw on for applications?

Eligible participants may complete a lightweight survey. Invite a subset for a 30–45 minute semi-structured interview. Target 10–20 surveys and 4–6 interviews.

## Recruitment message

> We are studying how technical-job applicants organize past experience and tailor application materials. Eligible senior undergraduates or postgraduates who apply to at least five software, AI, or data roles per week and have at least three substantial experiences may complete an anonymous short survey. Some volunteers may join an optional interview. For the interview only, you may optionally walk through a de-identified past resume version and JD; do not upload or send either file. We can use fictional materials instead. Do not send a resume, phone number, ID, or other sensitive information.

## Exclusions and data minimization

- Do not recruit people outside the target workflow as main-study participants.
- Do not collect names, student IDs, phone numbers, personal email addresses, resumes, identity documents, employer login details, or application records.
- The facilitator assigns anonymous study IDs such as `P01` only after consent.
- If scheduling requires a contact channel, keep it outside the research notes and delete it after scheduling.
